Welcome to Royal Servant Church

At Royal Servant Church, we are a community of believers united by a simple, powerful truth: Jesus Christ is Lord.


We are a non-sectarian church, meaning we do not bind ourselves to human traditions, denominational labels, or rigid man-made divisions. Instead, our heart, our message, and our entire focus are centered on one name above all names: Jesus, our God Almighty.
