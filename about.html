About Us

At Royal Servant Church, we are a community of believers united by a simple, powerful truth: Jesus Christ is Lord.

We are a non-sectarian church, meaning we do not bind ourselves to human traditions, denominational labels, or rigid man-made divisions. Instead, our heart, our message, and our entire focus are centered on one name above all names: Jesus, our God Almighty.

Who We Are

Christ-Centered & Non-Sectarian

Jesus is the foundation, the head, and the heartbeat of everything we do. We don't preach a denomination; we preach Christ and Him crucified, welcoming all who seek a genuine relationship with God.

Firmly Bible-Based

We believe that the Holy Scriptures are the inspired, infallible Word of God. The Bible is our absolute and final authority for faith, life, and practice, guiding everything we believe and do.

Uniting Believers

We gather around the pure truth of God's Word, free from religious barriers, seeking to walk together in love and unity.
